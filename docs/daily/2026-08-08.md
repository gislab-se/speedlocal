  preferred target is all authoritative mapped lake polygons without a
  buffer. A large-lake threshold is acceptable only as an explicitly partial
  interim policy after sensitivity analysis.
- Kartverket's administrative-units data is the preferred boundary candidate,
  but its selected snapshot/effective date must be pinned rather than silently
  tracking updates. NVE's lake database is the preferred lake candidate because
  it supplies polygon geometry, a national lake id, and declared area; a local
  checksum-pinned snapshot must replace any live runtime dependency.

## Known risks

- Expanding the R7 domain can require new landscape and five-group observations
  for cells that were absent from the accepted 13,735-cell reference.
- A lake area threshold without stable feature identity or dissolve semantics
  can change when source tiling/generalization changes.
- Removing inland water changes denominators, potential area, map geometry,
  allocation capacity, and scenario shortage; it is not a cosmetic map edit.
- Reusing one processed mask as both region definition and technology policy
  obscures provenance and makes later offshore/floating technologies unsafe.
